✦ Numerology as a field of study…

Numerology explores contemplation through numbers. It is ancient knowledge used by early civilisations for many purposes. Its earliest sources come from Egypt, Mesopotamia, India and Babylonia, including Egyptian papyri and Chaldean clay tablets.

Its origins are difficult to establish. Some believe this knowledge was left to humanity by the Atlanteans. It has existed since time immemorial. Surviving works by Greek philosophers Democritus, Plato and Pythagoras show their interest in these subjects.

Pythagoras, from the Greek island of Samos, was a traveller, philosopher and mathematician also drawn to esoteric studies. He established a school of esoteric knowledge and personal transformation in Italy, laying foundations for modern Western numerology.

✦ Numerology portrait

Numbers are all around us and within us. Each has positive and negative qualities, revealing character, personality and human experience. Our birth date and names have a strong influence on our lives.

A numerology portrait reveals personality traits, talents, abilities and skills waiting for the right moment to emerge. It shows professional aptitudes and helps choose work aligned with one's calling. Numerology describes the social context of our existence and indicates what is closest to our Soul at a given time.

The portrait highlights weaknesses and sensitive parts of the body needing greater care. It shows challenges and ways to meet them, and the rewards of living in harmony with one's Life Plan. It also explores emotions, partnerships and relationships.

A portrait for a young child guides parents in supporting development in line with the child's Destiny. For teenagers and young adults, it helps with choosing education suited to future work. Numerology can also help adults experiencing professional burnout.

A numerology portrait contains the life programme a person was born with and the qualities that lead towards its fulfilment.

✦ How to calculate your Life Path

For example, if your date of birth is: 10.12.1984, calculate it as follows:

1+0+1+2+1+9+8+4=26=2+6=8…

In this example, the person's Life Path has the numerological vibration „8”. The Life Path describes the goal we move towards and our strongest drives and qualities.

✦ Important numbers in numerology

Numerology is based on nine principal numbers: 1–9, which form all compound numbers. It also considers the meaning of Master Numbers: 11, 22, 33, 44 and Karmic Numbers: 13, 14, 16, 19, 26.

Master Numbers — 11, 22, 33, 44 ⌄

High Master Vibrations occur in people who gained deep spiritual knowledge and understanding of universal truths in previous lives. A Master Vibration calls for work in service of humanity's consciousness.

Master Number 11: sharing knowledge, inspiration, diplomacy, support, foresight and clairvoyance.

Master Number 22: the power to create, balance between spirit and matter, and work for the community — the “Architect of the Universe”.

Master Number 33: universal love, service to ideals, dedication, sharing wisdom and Spiritual Light.

Master Number 44: perseverance, professionalism, great achievements and innovation, spirit over matter and a gift for self-healing.

Karmic Numbers — 13, 14, 16, 19, 26 ⌄

Karma is the law of cause and effect: the consequence of choices and actions. Our present life results from experiences, deeds and intentions in previous lives.

Karmic 13 (work): learning responsibility and effort; possible lessons in material and professional life.

Karmic 14 (freedom): learning freedom without hurting others; working with boundaries, duties and responsibility.

Karmic 16 (relationships): lessons in partnerships and family relationships, with attention to responsibility and loyalty.

Karmic 19 (power): learning respect for others; possible lessons in dependence and acceptance.

Karmic 26 (body): the body as a teacher; balance in caring for health, movement and moderation.

Other important numbers — 17, 27, 41 ⌄

17: The Number of Cosmic Protection (personal protection), a “Lucky Star”, intuition, clairvoyance and success.

27: The Number of Spiritual Power: sensitivity combined with intelligence, a mission to help and inner wisdom.

41: Cosmic Protection in professional life, support in social matters, responsibility and ethics.
