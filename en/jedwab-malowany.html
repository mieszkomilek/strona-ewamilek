Silk painting course

A magical journey into silk

My silk painting course is for women who would like to create something unique and truly their own. Join me on a magical journey into silk.

During the course, you will discover different silk painting techniques, design and create your own unique silk scarf, and explore the exciting technique of painting on suspended silk. I will introduce you to the wonderful world of silk painting.

What if you gave it a try? What if you fell in love with your own creation on silk? Perhaps now is the perfect time to try silk painting.

P.S. Coffee, tea and something sweet are provided during the workshop.

✦ THE LEGEND OF SILK

According to Chinese tradition, silk was discovered in the garden of Emperor Huang Di, who lived from 2698 to 2598 BCE. The legend says he asked his wife Xi Lingshi to investigate which pest was damaging the mulberry trees. She noticed white larvae spinning shiny cocoons.

One cocoon accidentally fell into boiling water, revealing a delicate thread that Xi Lingshi wound onto a spool. According to legend, this is how she discovered the secret of silk production.
