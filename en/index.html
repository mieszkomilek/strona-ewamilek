Let's get to know each other

Life is my greatest teacher.

For many years I have combined creativity with inner growth. Painting is my passion, and Mandalas and Angels are an important part of that journey. Through numerology, I help people explore their talents, possibilities and life cycles.

I have been a Vedic Art teacher since 2010. My studies include psychotronic psychology and fine arts. In 2018, I published a poetry collection, “Written in the Soul”.

Vedic Art in a women's circle

Create without judgement. Focus on the process.

Vedic Art® is a personal development method created by Swedish artist Curt Källman. Ewa's workshops last six days and cover the whole of Level I or Level II.

01

Come as you are

You do not need to “know how to paint”. What matters is curiosity about the process and a willingness to create.

02

Explore the 17 Principles

The workshop guides you through the Vedic Art Principles at a calm, creative pace.

03

Create in your own way

Choose canvases, paints and tools that suit your own process and expression.
