About me

Let's get to know each other

Life, for me…

Life is my greatest teacher. I grow through relationships, the needs of my heart and a consciously chosen path of development.

Life is my greatest teacher. I grow through human relationships, guided by my heart. My soul shows me which path to take. For many years I have consciously chosen my own process of growth.

I have met wonderful teachers and masters who helped my life find its true tone. I enjoy inspiring people to develop themselves. Through numerology, I show how valuable each of us is and how many talents, abilities and possibilities we can use to live better and more happily.

Painting is my passion, and Mandalas and Angels guide me. A Mandala contains an image of the Soul and all its beauty. Creating a Mandala for you activates a process of growth. When painting Mandalas and Angels, I focus on the flow of understanding connecting me with that Soul, creating a pure message between channels of higher consciousness.

In the studio

Painting at the easelPainting at the tableWorking on a large painting

Yoga has been a major part of my life for years. It helps me concentrate and enter deep meditation while remaining physically active, painting what I am called to paint to awaken the Soul's growth.

I graduated from the School of Psychotronic Psychology, broadening my esoteric and psychological knowledge and awakening my intuition. Intuition now guides me towards my Life Destiny. It led me to choose a thesis topic in my own field: “Colours in the Mandala as a Tool for Colour Therapy”.

VEDIC ART, which I teach, complements and unifies my message to others: “Always follow your heart and listen to what it tells you”.

I graduated from the School of Fine Arts in Bydgoszcz, which broadened my knowledge of painting and art history.

My interests span the physical and spiritual worlds. I work with my subtle bodies and express myself through painting, writing, yoga and sharing spiritual and numerological knowledge.

In 2018, I published a poetry collection: “Written in the Soul”. I am also a wife, mother and grandmother. A vegetarian who loves nature and her garden, travelling across Planet Earth and through spiritual dimensions.
